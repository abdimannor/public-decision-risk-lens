Advisory discussion aid. This early prototype matches visible words and phrases. It can miss risks and it will produce false positives. It does not decide anything and does not assess people. Every flag needs a human reviewer.

Public Decision Risk Lens prototype

Paste a public decision document or load a .txt or PDF file. PDF text is read in this browser and every passage keeps its page number. Passages that contain trigger terms are highlighted and listed by category for human review.

1. Document

Your text, decisions and notes are saved in this browser only (local storage) so they survive a reload. Use Clear review to remove them.

Your own rules

Add a word or phrase you want to watch for. Your rules run next to the built-in taxonomy, are marked Reviewer-added in flags, exports and the printed report, and are saved in this browser. Share them as a JSON rule pack.

The result shows which flags appear, disappear or persist. A flag that disappears may mean the wording changed, not that a concern was addressed: read both passages.
